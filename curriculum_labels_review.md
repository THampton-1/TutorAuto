# Curriculum labels & sub-skills — review

**What this is.** A proposed short `label` and `sub-skills` breakdown for every one of
the 97 Edexcel GCSE Higher nodes, produced by the spec-point → teacher-label technique.
You review and hand-edit; I load your final version into the database.

**The technique (for reference, and for the A-level pass later):**
1. Strip the objective verb, keep the mathematical object — the label is a noun phrase.
2. Collapse enumerations and bundles to the umbrella heading a teacher files it under.
3. Coordinate siblings so labels disambiguate *within a module* (decided module by module).
4. Use the canonical UK classroom name, verified — never an ad-hoc paraphrase.
5. Sub-skills = distinct teaching sessions. Usually the spec's semicolon clauses; for a
   long enumeration, group the list into teaching units, don't split it item by item.
6. Atomic nodes keep a single sub-skill (= themselves).

**How to edit.** Change any `Label:` or sub-skill line freely. Delete a sub-skill to merge,
add one to split. `⚠ Grain check` marks nodes where I think the breakdown is debatable —
look hardest at those. Don't touch the `### Nx` headings or the `Spec:` lines (those are
the verbatim source of truth and the key I load against).

## N — Number

### N1
- **Label:** Ordering numbers & inequality symbols
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Ordering integers, decimals & fractions
  - Inequality symbols (=, ≠, <, >, ≤, ≥)
- **Spec:** order positive and negative integers, decimals and fractions; use the symbols =, ≠, <, >, ≤, ≥

### N2
- **Label:** Numerical operations & place value
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Four operations (incl. negatives, fractions, mixed numbers)
  - Place value
- **Spec:** apply the four operations, including formal written methods, to integers, decimals and simple fractions (proper and improper), and mixed numbers – all both positive and negative; understand and use place value

### N3
- **Label:** BIDMAS & inverses
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Inverse operations
  - Order of operations (BIDMAS)
- **Spec:** recognise and use relationships between operations, including inverse operations; use conventional notation for priority of operations, including brackets, powers, roots and reciprocals

### N4
- **Label:** Primes, factors & multiples
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Primes, factors & multiples
  - HCF & LCM
  - Prime factorisation
- **Spec:** use the concepts and vocabulary of prime numbers, factors (divisors), multiples, common factors, common multiples, highest common factor, lowest common multiple, prime factorisation, including using product notation and the unique factorisation theorem

### N5  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Systematic listing & the product rule for counting
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Systematic listing strategies
  - Product rule for counting
- **Spec:** apply systematic listing strategies, including use of the product rule for counting

### N6  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Powers & roots
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Powers & roots (square, cube, higher)
  - Estimating powers & roots
- **Spec:** use positive integer powers and associated real roots (square, cube and higher), recognise powers of 2, 3, 4, 5; estimate powers and roots of any given positive number

### N7
- **Label:** Indices & roots
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Integer & fractional indices
  - Calculating with roots
- **Spec:** calculate with roots, and with integer and fractional indices

### N8  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Surds
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Exact values (surds & π)
  - Simplifying surds
  - Rationalising denominators
- **Spec:** calculate exactly with fractions, surds and multiples of π; simplify surd expressions involving squares (e.g. √12 = √(4 × 3) = √4 × √3 = 2√3) and rationalise denominators

### N9
- **Label:** Standard form
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Standard form
- **Spec:** calculate with and interpret standard form A × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is an integer

### N10  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Fractions & decimals (incl. recurring)
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Decimals ↔ fractions
  - Recurring decimals ↔ fractions
- **Spec:** work interchangeably with terminating decimals and their corresponding fractions; change recurring decimals into their corresponding fractions and vice versa

### N11
- **Label:** Fractions in ratio problems
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Fractions in ratio problems
- **Spec:** identify and work with fractions in ratio problems

### N12
- **Label:** Fractions & percentages as operators
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Fractions & percentages as operators
- **Spec:** interpret fractions and percentages as operators

### N13
- **Label:** Standard & compound units
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Standard & compound units
- **Spec:** use standard units of mass, length, time, money and other measures (including standard compound measures) using decimal quantities where appropriate

### N14
- **Label:** Estimation & approximation
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Estimation & approximation
- **Spec:** estimate answers; check calculations using approximation and estimation, including answers obtained using technology

### N15
- **Label:** Rounding & error intervals
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Rounding (decimal places & significant figures)
  - Error intervals (truncation/rounding)
- **Spec:** round numbers and measures to an appropriate degree of accuracy; use inequality notation to specify simple error intervals due to truncation or rounding

### N16  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Upper & lower bounds
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Upper & lower bounds
- **Spec:** apply and interpret limits of accuracy, including upper and lower bounds

## A — Algebra

### A1
- **Label:** Algebraic notation
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Algebraic notation
- **Spec:** use and interpret algebraic manipulation, including: ab in place of a × b; 3y in place of y + y + y and 3 × y; a² in place of a × a, a³ in place of a × a × a, a²b in place of a × a × b; a/b in place of a ÷ b; coefficients written as fractions rather than as decimals; brackets

### A2
- **Label:** Substitution
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Substitution into formulae & expressions
- **Spec:** substitute numerical values into formulae and expressions, including scientific formulae

### A3
- **Label:** Algebraic vocabulary
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Expressions, equations, formulae, identities, terms & factors
- **Spec:** understand and use the concepts and vocabulary of expressions, equations, formulae, identities, inequalities, terms and factors

### A4  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Simplifying & factorising
- **⚠ Grain check:** heaviest algebra node — 6 sub-skills below. These are genuinely 6 separate lessons, but if you track at the "factorising" level rather than splitting easy/hard quadratics, merge the last two.
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Collecting like terms
  - Expanding brackets (single & double)
  - Factorising with common factors
  - Factorising quadratics (x² + bx + c, incl. difference of two squares)
  - Factorising harder quadratics (ax² + bx + c)
  - Index laws in algebra
- **Spec:** simplify and manipulate algebraic expressions (including those involving surds) by: collecting like terms; multiplying a single term over a bracket; taking out common factors; expanding products of two or more binomials; factorising quadratic expressions of the form x² + bx + c, including the difference of two squares; factorising quadratic expressions of the form ax² + bx + c; simplifying expressions involving sums, products and powers, including the laws of indices

### A5
- **Label:** Formulae & changing the subject
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Using standard formulae
  - Rearranging / changing the subject
- **Spec:** understand and use standard mathematical formulae; rearrange formulae to change the subject

### A6
- **Label:** Identities & algebraic proof
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Equation vs identity
  - Algebraic proof
- **Spec:** know the difference between an equation and an identity; argue mathematically to show algebraic expressions are equivalent, and use algebra to support and construct arguments and proofs

### A7  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Functions (inverse & composite)
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Function notation (inputs & outputs)
  - Inverse functions
  - Composite functions
- **Spec:** where appropriate, interpret simple expressions as functions with inputs and outputs; interpret the reverse process as the 'inverse function'; interpret the succession of two functions as a 'composite function'

### A8
- **Label:** Coordinates
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Coordinates in all four quadrants
- **Spec:** work with coordinates in all four quadrants

### A9  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Equation of a straight line
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Plotting straight-line graphs
  - Parallel & perpendicular lines
  - Finding the equation of a line
- **Spec:** plot graphs of equations that correspond to straight-line graphs in the coordinate plane; use the form y = mx + c to identify parallel and perpendicular lines; find the equation of the line through two given points or through one point with a given gradient

### A10
- **Label:** Gradients & intercepts
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Interpreting gradients & intercepts
- **Spec:** identify and interpret gradients and intercepts of linear functions graphically and algebraically

### A11  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Quadratic graphs & completing the square
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Roots, intercepts & turning points (graphically)
  - Turning points by completing the square
- **Spec:** identify and interpret roots, intercepts, turning points of quadratic functions graphically; deduce roots algebraically and turning points by completing the square

### A12  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Graphs of standard functions
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Linear & quadratic graphs
  - Cubic & reciprocal graphs
  - Exponential graphs
  - Trig graphs (sin / cos / tan)
- **Spec:** recognise, sketch and interpret graphs of linear functions, quadratic functions, simple cubic functions, the reciprocal function y = 1/x with x ≠ 0, exponential functions y = kˣ for positive values of k, and the trigonometric functions y = sin x, y = cos x and y = tan x for angles of any size

### A13
- **Label:** Transformations of graphs
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Translations of graphs
  - Reflections of graphs
- **Spec:** sketch translations and reflections of a given function

### A14  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Real-life graphs
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Graphs in real contexts
  - Kinematics graphs (distance, speed, acceleration)
- **Spec:** plot and interpret graphs (including reciprocal graphs and exponential graphs) and graphs of non-standard functions in real contexts to find approximate solutions to problems such as simple kinematic problems involving distance, speed and acceleration

### A15
- **Label:** Gradients & areas under graphs
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Gradient of a graph (rate of change)
  - Area under a graph
  - Distance-time & velocity-time graphs
- **Spec:** calculate or estimate gradients of graphs and areas under graphs (including quadratic and other non-linear graphs), and interpret results in cases such as distance-time graphs, velocity-time graphs and graphs in financial contexts (this does not include calculus)

### A16
- **Label:** Equation of a circle & tangents
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Equation of a circle (centre at origin)
  - Tangent to a circle
- **Spec:** recognise and use the equation of a circle with centre at the origin; find the equation of a tangent to a circle at a given point

### A17
- **Label:** Linear equations
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Solving linear equations (incl. unknown on both sides)
- **Spec:** solve linear equations in one unknown algebraically (including those with the unknown on both sides of the equation); find approximate solutions using a graph

### A18
- **Label:** Quadratic equations
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Solving by factorising
  - Solving with the quadratic formula
- **Spec:** solve quadratic equations (including those that require rearrangement) algebraically by factorising and by using the quadratic formula; find approximate solutions using a graph

### A19  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** Simultaneous equations
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Linear simultaneous equations
  - Linear / quadratic simultaneous equations
- **Spec:** solve two simultaneous equations in two variables (linear/linear or linear/quadratic) algebraically; find approximate solutions using a graph

### A20
- **Label:** Iteration
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Solving equations by iteration
- **Spec:** find approximate solutions to equations numerically using iteration

### A21
- **Label:** Forming & solving equations
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Forming expressions / equations from a context
  - Solving & interpreting the solution
- **Spec:** translate simple situations or procedures into algebraic expressions or formulae; derive an equation (or two simultaneous equations), solve the equation(s) and interpret the solution

### A22
- **Label:** Inequalities
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Linear inequalities
  - Quadratic inequalities
  - Representing solutions (number line, set notation, graph)
- **Spec:** solve linear inequalities in one or two variable(s), and quadratic inequalities in one variable; represent the solution set on a number line, using set notation and on a graph

### A23
- **Label:** Generating sequences
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Term-to-term & position-to-term rules
- **Spec:** generate terms of a sequence from either a term-to-term or a position-to-term rule

### A24
- **Label:** Types of sequence
- **⚠ Grain check:** 4 sub-skills; merge the special-sequences one if you don't teach those separately.
- **Sub-skills:**
  - Special sequences (triangular, square, cube, Fibonacci)
  - Arithmetic progressions
  - Geometric progressions
  - Quadratic sequences
- **Spec:** recognise and use sequences of triangular, square and cube numbers, simple arithmetic progressions, Fibonacci type sequences, quadratic sequences, and simple geometric progressions and other sequences

### A25  *(stretch)*
- **Label:** nth term
- **Sub-skills:**
  - nth term of linear sequences
  - nth term of quadratic sequences
- **Spec:** deduce expressions to calculate the nth term of linear and quadratic sequences
